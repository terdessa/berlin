Current direction:

Sentinel is a voice-first retail security copilot for the telli + ai-coustics track.

It watches CCTV, alerts a guard through an earpiece, hears noisy spoken commands using ai-coustics + telli, opens the relevant video, and writes a structured interaction record for every exchange. Failures get a rich diagnostic record (raw + enhanced audio, NISQA scores, command candidates, failure classification, natural-language explanation). The whole session ships as a submission/interactions.json corpus that can be shared with the ai-coustics team as evaluation or training data.

Voice runtime: telli (track host). Audio enhancement: ai-coustics via livekit/plugins-ai-coustics-python. Objective metric: NISQA v2.

Side challenge focus: Entire for review/error tasks (push at least one corpus record), Aikido if quick.
