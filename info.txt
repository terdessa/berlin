---

## 🧠 Core Idea

**Sentinel** is a real-time multimodal AI system for retail loss-prevention awareness. It analyzes **live shop camera streams** to detect high-risk situations and alert humans instantly.

It focuses on understanding **what is happening right now**, not just recording it for later.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Retail stores already capture huge amounts of information through cameras, but:

* most systems are passive and only reviewed after incidents
* human operators cannot monitor many feeds effectively
* high-risk retail moments are often noticed too late

---

## 💡 Solution

Sentinel continuously processes **shop camera input** and identifies observable retail situations that may require attention.

When a possible loss-prevention review moment happens, it:

* generates a real-time alert
* summarizes what is happening
* provides context to help a human decide what to do next

---

## ⚙️ What the system detects

* item taken from shelf and placed into pocket or bag
* concealment-like hand motion
* checkout bypass
* self-checkout mismatch
* unusual movement near high-value items

The demo should pick one controlled retail review event and make the alert path feel real.

---

## 🖥️ UI Concept (this is where you should shine)

Focus your demo here.

### Main dashboard:

* grid of live camera feeds
* system runs in background

### When something happens:

* feed is highlighted
* alert card appears with:

  * short description
  * confidence level
  * timestamp
* short video/audio clip replay

### Actions:

* acknowledge
* send floor associate
* ignore

👉 This makes it feel like a real product.

---

## 🧩 Key Features

* real-time video + audio understanding
* instant alert generation
* human-in-the-loop decision making
* simple, actionable summaries
* works on existing retail camera infrastructure

---

## ⚖️ Safety & Positioning

* no facial recognition
* no identity tracking
* no automated decisions
* always requires human confirmation

---

## 💰 Business Value (keep high-level)

* faster review of high-risk retail situations
* fewer missed events
* better use of existing systems
* reduced shrinkage and camera-monitoring overload

---

## 🎯 One-line pitch

> Sentinel turns passive video and audio streams into real-time awareness.

---

## 🧠 Strategic positioning (important)

If someone asks “what exactly do you detect?”:

Say:

> “Sentinel detects observable retail sequences that may require human review, such as shelf-to-pocket movement, shelf-to-bag movement, checkout bypass, self-checkout mismatch, or unusual high-value shelf activity.”

This keeps the product B2B and human-reviewed while avoiding theft accusations or identity tracking.
