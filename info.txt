Sentinel: voice-first retail security copilot.

Core demo:

1. CCTV feed shows review-worthy shelf-to-pocket behavior.
2. Sentinel says in the guard's earpiece: "Aisle 5 requires review."
3. Guard says in noisy store audio: "Open aisle five."
4. ai-coustics enhances the audio.
5. Voice layer understands the command.
6. Sentinel opens the video and logs the context.
7. If voice understanding fails, Sentinel creates an error report with video, assistant prompt, raw audio, enhanced audio, transcript attempt, command guess, confidence, and correction.
