# Pitch

## One-Liner

Sentinel turns passive video and audio streams into real-time awareness.

## Core Promise

Sentinel reduces the time between a high-risk retail event happening and a human security operator noticing it.

## Short Pitch

Retailers already have cameras across aisles, entrances, exits, and checkout areas, but most of that information is passive. A security operator may need to watch 20 cameras at the same time, so high-risk moments are easy to miss.

Sentinel analyzes live retail camera feeds, detects observable situations that may deserve attention, and highlights the relevant camera with evidence, summaries, and human review actions.

## Longer Pitch Skeleton

1. Store cameras record everything, but awareness still depends on humans noticing the right moment.
2. In busy retail environments, one operator may need to watch many cameras at once.
3. Sentinel monitors live camera streams in real time.
4. When observable behavior suggests a possible loss-prevention review moment, it highlights the relevant camera with evidence and context.
5. Operators stay in control: they can acknowledge, watch live, send a floor associate, create an incident record, or dismiss.
6. The result is faster awareness without facial recognition, identity tracking, automated accusation, or automated enforcement.

## Demo Narration Skeleton

We start with a live operations dashboard showing a shop floor plan with monitored cameras.

Sentinel is analyzing the feeds in the background.

A high-risk retail sequence occurs in one aisle: an item is taken from a shelf and appears to be placed into a pocket or bag. The system makes the camera marker pulse red and creates an alert.

The alert explains the observable behavior, how confident the system is, and shows the evidence clip.

The operator reviews the alert and chooses an action, such as acknowledge, watch live, send a floor associate, ignore, or create an incident record.

This turns existing retail cameras from passive recording tools into real-time attention guidance for security teams.

## Demo Build Framing

For the hackathon demo, the system can show many previewable shop camera feeds to communicate coverage, while one real phone livestream demonstrates live AI detection. Most feeds can be looped videos representing retail cameras. This keeps the demo reliable while still proving the core intelligent alert path.

## Current Positioning

Sentinel should be positioned as a B2B retail loss-prevention awareness product. The pitch should emphasize that Sentinel reduces camera-monitoring overload by turning store camera streams into actionable human review, without accusing anyone of theft.
